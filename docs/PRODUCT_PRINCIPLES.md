# Product Principles

These are the core principles that guide every product decision for DropIn.

- Activity First
- Search First
- One Decision Per Screen
- Reduce Thinking, Not Information
- Comfortable Before Beautiful
- Real-Time Confidence
- Don't imply certainty when the data cannot support it.
- Simplicity over feature completeness.
- The MVP focuses exclusively on Drop-in activities, not registered programs.
- The Results state helps users choose.
- The Quick Action Sheet helps users verify information through official sources.
- When in doubt, link to the official source instead of recreating or interpreting it.
